Questions

Frequently asked questions

Does Travisa guarantee visa approval?

No. Travisa provides processing and documentation assistance. The final decision is made by the relevant immigration or consular authority.

Are government visa fees included in your service fee?

Fees can vary by destination, nationality and visa type. Travisa will communicate applicable government/visa charges and service charges before processing.

Can I apply completely online?

Yes, you can start your enquiry online. Depending on the destination and application channel, additional documents, biometrics or in-person steps may be required.

How early should I apply?

Apply well before your intended travel date. Processing times vary, so Travisa will advise based on the destination and application type.
